# Dr. Tingting (Rachel) Chung

Dr. Tingting (Rachel) Chung is Associate Teaching Professor in the Heinz College of Public Policy and Information Systems, Carnegie Mellon University, and a former Clinical Full Professor of Business Analytics at William & Mary. She holds a Ph.D. in Business Administration/Management Information Systems, a Ph.D. in Psychology, and a Master of Science in Information Science, all from the University of Pittsburgh.

To explain how AI works to the broader audience, Dr. Chung has published the children's book [AI the Magic Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DKCR7QPW?lv=shuf&channelId=500&plpRedirect=mhFallback) with former William & Mary students, and given a [TEDx Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kVLwAB9v338) at William & Mary based on this book. Dr. Chung has also authored the textbook [AI for Business](https://www.prospectpressvt.com/textbooks/chung-ai-for-business) with former William & Mary student Steven Hojnicki. In 2026, Dr. Chung gave the campus-wide [Tack Lecture](https://www.wm.edu/sites/facultylecture/past/2026-spring/) at William & Mary to illustrate AI's roots in the liberal arts.

Dr. Chung's research has been published in [Journal of Management](https://journals.sagepub.com/eprint/6BQFG7RKCGDERCHWB573/full), [Journal of Association for Information Systems](https://aisel.aisnet.org/jais/), [Communications of the ACM](http://cacm.acm.org/), [Journal of Managerial Psychology](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/JMP-07-2017-0228), [International Journal of Production Economics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925527316303425), [Journal of Information & Knowledge Management](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wsi/jikmxx/v16y2017i04ns0219649217500332.html), [AIS Transactions on HCI](https://aisel.aisnet.org/thci/), and [Omega](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305048306000934). She has also given numerous presentations at international conferences, including [EAAI](https://eaai-conf.github.io/), [ICIS](http://aisel.aisnet.org/icis/), [ICAIF](https://ai-finance.org/), [AoM](http://aom.org/), [INFORMS](https://www.informs.org/), and [SIGCSE](https://sigcse2020.sigcse.org/). Dr. Chung's research has been supported by [ACFE Research Institute (ARI)](https://www.acfe.com/ACFE-Research-Institute/), [Blockchain Lab of William & Mary](https://www.wm.edu/offices/global-research/projects/blockchain-lab/index.php), and [National Security Agency](https://www.nsa.gov/).

Dr. Chung has completed a visiting scholarship at [Vietnam National University – International School](https://vnu.edu.vn/eng/?C2246/N14000/International-School-(VNU-IS).htm), and has received the [IBM Faculty Award in the Cognitive Computing category](https://www.research.ibm.com/university/awards/faculty_innovation_2016.shtml), [Community Partner of the Year Award, Houston Methodist Hospital, on behalf of INFORMS](https://pubsonline.informs.org/do/10.1287/LYTX.2019.04.18n/full/), [Faculty Excellence Award from the Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA) program](https://mason.wm.edu/news/2020/both-distanced-and-together.php?fbclid=IwAR1IAUjYqqxBvURrdXX0pRPFfLon5X2nRGvj8FqIWM0jYB_eeSSdFHAPkG8), the online MSBA program, Page Teaching Award for the MSBA program, and MBA Class of 1997 Faculty Award for Innovative Use of Technology, at William & Mary.
